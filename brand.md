## Gradients

Two restrained gradients are available as `bg-brand-bg` and `bg-brand-accent`.

- `bg-brand-bg` is a low-contrast atmospheric wash for a hero or phase backdrop.
- `bg-brand-accent` is reserved for a thin accent, signature reveal, or rare hero focal point.
- Never place body copy on the accent gradient.
- Never use both gradients prominently on the same screen.
- Never animate either gradient.

## Visual language

- Compose screens like theatrical title cards: one focal statement, strong negative space, and a grounded action region.
- Prefer flat surfaces and one-pixel borders. Avoid stacked shadows, glass cards, neon glows, and excessive rounding.
- Gold indicates focus, agency, and verified completion. Red is reserved for death, destructive actions, and actual danger.
- Atmospheric texture may use subtle grain, silhouettes, or line work. It must not reduce text contrast.
- Transitions should feel intentional and brief. Signature reveals may be dramatic but remain skippable.

## Tone and voice

Use concise, tense, human language. The narrator may be evocative; controls must remain literal. “The village sleeps” can set the scene, while the action stays “Lock choice.”

Avoid blockchain jargon in the primary flow, hype language, exclamation marks, generic fantasy exposition, and fake urgency. Translate proving and finality into “Sealing your choice,” “Submitting,” and “Choice locked,” with technical details available on demand.

Voice example:

> Someone here is lying. Read the room, protect your secret, and decide who survives the night.

## Usage rules

**Do:**

- Use semantic tokens such as `bg-background`, `text-foreground`, and `border-border`.
- Keep spacing on the 4px grid and touch targets at least 44×44px.
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for changing values.
- Test dark, light, high-contrast, and reduced-motion states.
- Use one clear primary action per phase.

**Don't:**

- Hard-code color values in components.
- Use `transition-all`, glowing effects, or multiple decorative gradients.
- Use gold as decoration everywhere; scarcity gives it meaning.
- Hide essential actions behind hover or color alone.
- Expose private roles, actions, or ballots in URLs, analytics, logs, or notifications.
